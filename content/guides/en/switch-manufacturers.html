Cherry MX switches come from Cherry, Gateron switches from Gateron, Kailh switches from Kailh, Outemu switches from Outemu. Akko is a brand, and its switches are built by KTT, TTC, HMX and Huano. Switch names often hide that split between factories that build switches and brands that sell them. For what switches do, start with the guide to keyboard switches.

What do Cherry, Gateron, Kailh, TTC and Outemu each make?

Cherry. The company was founded in 1953 by Walter Lorain Cherry in Highland Park, Illinois, and sits in Auerbach in der Oberpfalz, Germany. The MX switch was developed and patented in the early 1980s and first marketed around 1985. Cherry's switch page lists the MX2A line at these stated forces: Red 45, Blue 50, Brown 45, Black 60, Silent Red 45 and Speed Silver 45, in cN, Cherry's unit. The Multipoint switches are inductive analog switches that work only on dedicated Multipoint PCBs. Switch specs explains cN and gf.

Gateron. Gateron Electronic Technology designs and produces micro switches and keyboard switches, including mechanical, optical and magnetic, with in-house mold making, in Huizhou, Guangdong. Its own about page says it also makes switches for other keyboard brands. Gateron's catalog in the petitdoigt switch database runs to 241 entries across 135 linear, 42 tactile and 24 clicky switches, plus 40 silent ones.

Kailh. Kailh is the switch brand of Dongguan City Kaihua Electronics, founded in 1990. The range includes Box switches, Speed switches, Choc low-profile switches for thin boards and magnetic switches. The petitdoigt switch database holds 89 Kailh switches and lists Box Red 45 gf (linear), Box Brown 45 gf (tactile), Box White 45 gf and Box Jade 50 gf (both clicky).

TTC. TTC is the switch brand of Huizhou Trantek Electronics, founded in 1998, which also makes mouse switches, encoders and connectors. Its keyboard line covers MX-style mechanical and Hall-effect magnetic switches, with 75 entries in the petitdoigt switch database, 63 of them linear.

Outemu. Outemu belongs to Dongguan Gaote Electronics in Tangxia, Dongguan. Its Large Buckle MX family includes Outemu Blue (clicky, 50 gf), Brown (tactile, 53 gf), Red (linear, 46 gf) and Black (linear, 65 gf), and its silent line includes Peach V3 and Lemon V3.

How do you choose between makers?

Force, travel, mount and type tell you more than a maker's name: two Gateron switches can differ more than a Gateron and a Kailh of the same type.
